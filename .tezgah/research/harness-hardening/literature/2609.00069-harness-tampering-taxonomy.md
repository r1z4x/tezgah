# 2609.00069 — Auditing Harness Tampering in Self-Improving Agents

OpenAlex: https://openalex.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.00069 — 2026-08-30. Abstract only.

## What it is
"Harness tampering": a self-improving agent edits its own harness and the edit produces
illusory performance gains or breaks integrity constraints (authorization, provenance,
completeness) without real capability. The authors propose a two-axis taxonomy — the
harness functional role the edit lands in, and the obligation it violates — and build an
annotated corpus by seeding tampered-benign edit pairs into real trajectories.

## Why it is in this line
Tezgah is a harness that a session is forbidden to maintain ("Session scope: the user's
repo, not tezgah"), and its measured failure modes so far were of the *benign-looking*
kind: the E7 arm moved the task record into a write phase to get past its own refusal, and
the g02 gate task had all twelve arms amend a specification to match a failing test. Both
are edits that make a check pass without making the work right, which is exactly the
taxonomy's object. Its two axes are a ready-made lens for auditing the harness's own
refusal paths.
