# Microsoft Turkish Localization Style Guide (52 pp)

**What it is and claims.** The decisive Turkish register source: `siz` is the default address and `sen` is reserved for young audiences and marketing taglines; the polite `-(y)InIz` verb form is explicitly rejected as an authoritative tone; word-for-word Turkish is named stiff and unnatural; the em dash cannot carry emphasis in Turkish. Publisher-authored and long-lived, so it is a house standard rather than a measurement.

**How it changes the skill.** Turkish needs an address-form rule and a calque rule, both of which the English skill has no slot for.

**Quality.** Grey: an organisation's own published guide, judged above; usable as a house standard, not as a measurement.
