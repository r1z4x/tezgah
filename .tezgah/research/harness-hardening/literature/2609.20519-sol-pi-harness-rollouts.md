# 2609.20519 — SoL-Pi: Recursively Scaling Auto-Research Loops for Efficient Agent Harness

NVIDIA / NTU / MIT. alphaXiv: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.20519 — 2026-09-17.
Abstract only.

## What it is
An RSI-inspired attack on the harness layer: scale auto-research loops across many
environments for harness rollouts, and keep the mechanisms that survive selection. Four
mechanisms survive and form SoL-Pi, spanning **action execution, context compaction,
observation handling, and de-duplication**. The claim is that improvements transfer beyond
their development setting.

## Why it is in this line
It is the industrial-scale version of the arm-bench lab: many arms, selection, keep what
survives. The transfer claim is the one this repository's lab could not reproduce (its
harness effect did not replicate across two model families), so the paper's method is
worth reading against the lab's negative result rather than as an endorsement of it.
