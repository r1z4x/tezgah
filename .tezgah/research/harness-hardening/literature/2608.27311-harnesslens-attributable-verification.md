# 2608.27311 — HarnessLens: Efficient Harness Evolution through Behavior-Aware Verification

alphaXiv: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.27311 — 2026-08-27. Abstract only (not read in full).

## What it is
A budget-aware framework for automated harness evolution: it explores the task space and
the user-configurable harness components together, derives candidate modifications from
execution trajectories, and **verifies each candidate selectively on behaviour-relevant
tasks behind an "attributable-evidence gate"**, rather than scoring every candidate on a
fixed task set. Reported across three agent harnesses and four benchmarks.

## Why it is in this line
It is the published form of the method this repository already runs by hand: the
arm-bench lab's pre-registered arms, cell splitting and per-cell attribution are the same
idea without the automation. The part to borrow is the *attributable-evidence gate* — a
candidate change is scored only on the tasks whose behaviour it can move — which is what
E2's zero-fire rules and the lab's saturated corpus (22 of 25 tasks saturated in the
two-host pilot) both argue for.

## Quality and limits
The abstract is the whole of what was read here; no methods, no numbers reproduced. Treat
every claim above as the paper's own summary, not as verified measurement.
