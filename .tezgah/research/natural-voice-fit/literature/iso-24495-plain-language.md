# ISO 24495-1:2023, Plain language - Part 1: governing principles and guidelines (ISO/TC 37)

**What it is and claims.** The only verified language-independent quality instrument found: the standard's own abstract says it applies to most, if not all, written languages, while providing examples only in English. The intended architecture matters as much as the text - an ISO standard plus a language-specific supplement, which is exactly what German did with DIN 8581-1.

**How it changes the skill.** It gives the language axis a backbone that needs no per-language invention: the principles are universal, and each language adds a supplement (its authority, its corpus, its marker evidence). A language with no supplement is named as uncovered rather than filled in by translating the English examples.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an academic-press record.
