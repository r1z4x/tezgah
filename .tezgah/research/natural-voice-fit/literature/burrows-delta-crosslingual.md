# Orekhov 2024, how Burrows' Delta works on medieval Chinese poetic texts (preprint)

**What it is and claims.** Delta applied with characters instead of words - the 100 most frequent characters, no tokenizer - clusters 20 Tang poets correctly, and the paper documents prior Delta use across English, Old English, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Russian, Arabic and a cross-language attribution comparison. It notes what changes in Chinese: no inflection, no spaces, and a function-word distribution unlike European languages.

**How it changes the skill.** A language-independent style-distance instrument whose only per-language adjustment is the token definition. This is what an editing skill can use to compare a draft against a corpus in the target language without inventing language-specific rules.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an academic-press record.
