# Zheng et al. 2023, judging LLM-as-a-judge (MT-Bench, arXiv 2306.05685)

**What it is and claims.** Judge position bias is large (GPT-4 consistency 65.0% on a swapped pair), verbosity is attacked successfully, self-enhancement appears, and swapping the pair order is the standard mitigation.

**How it changes the skill.** Each pairwise judgement runs twice with the arms swapped, and the generating model never judges its own arm.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
