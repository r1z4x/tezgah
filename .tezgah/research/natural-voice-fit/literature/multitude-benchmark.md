# Macko et al. 2023, MULTITuDE - a large-scale multilingual machine-generated text detection benchmark (EMNLP)

**What it is and claims.** 74,081 authentic and machine-generated texts in eleven languages (Arabic, Catalan, Czech, German, English, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese) from eight LLMs, testing whether detectors generalise to related and unrelated languages and to unseen models.

**How it changes the skill.** It is the closest thing to a cross-language checkpoint, and it is a detection benchmark rather than a comparison of translationese features - so the design may use it to sanity-check a language, not to source its markers.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an academic-press record.
