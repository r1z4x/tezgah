# TS Corpus sub-corpora and the Turkish National Corpus

**What it is and claims.** The reference corpora exist per register: Columns Corpus V2 (25,915 columns, 28M tokens, 2006-2017, balanced by author gender), TS Timeline (700M tokens of news, 1998-2016), Evrim Agaci (4.4M tokens of popular science), Abstract Corpus V2 (1M tokens of academic abstracts), against the TNC's 50M words in nine domains including columns and editorials.

**How it changes the skill.** The one-voice-for-every-reader defect gets a fix with a measured basis: a natural reference per register - column, news, popular science, academic - instead of a single idea of good Turkish.

**Quality.** Primary institutional source, not peer-reviewed; for the book and the guides this is the field's standing authority.
