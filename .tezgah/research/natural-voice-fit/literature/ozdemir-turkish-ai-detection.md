# Ozdemir 2026, from perceptions to evidence - detecting AI-generated content in Turkish (arXiv 2602.13504)

**What it is and claims.** The first Turkish AI-rewrite dataset (3,600 articles) with a Turkish BERT baseline at 97.08% F1, and the finding that Turkish morphology blocks the transfer of English detectors.

**How it changes the skill.** A Turkish marker set cannot be a translation of an English one; its features have to be Turkish morphological and syntactic facts.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
