# TDK Yabanci Sozlere Karsiliklar Kilavuzu

**What it is and claims.** The institutional frame for proposing Turkish equivalents, with the explicit instruction that a word already existing in Turkish should be preferred over the loanword, exemplified by pairs such as bodyguard -> koruma, prose -> surec, bold -> koyu, naturel -> dogal.

**How it changes the skill.** Gives the rejection of realize etmek-class constructions a formal basis rather than a preference.

**Quality.** Primary institutional source, not peer-reviewed; for the book and the guides this is the field's standing authority.
