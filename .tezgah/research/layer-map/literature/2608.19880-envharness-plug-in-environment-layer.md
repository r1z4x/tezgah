## What it is
Environment Harness is a programmable layer of plug-in components that wraps a *static*
environment to reshape its behaviour without modifying the underlying logic. Its three component
kinds are Setup, Rule and Link, applied through standard interfaces at the environment's
`reset()` and `step()`. The stated invariant is that every reshaped environment **retains its
original verifier** - the goal predicate and the benchmark's scoring are untouched. EnvRigger
automates synthesis: it treats the target policy as a black box, reads its trajectories,
synthesizes components aimed at diagnosed flaws, and validates them by fresh rollouts.

Reported: across five benchmarks in four domains, up to a 9.0-point improvement on held-out
instances with 9.8% fewer execution steps; on SWE-bench Verified, +2.70 success rate with -5.40
agent steps. It also claims a better optimization signal for reinforcement learning.

## Why it is in this line
EnvHarness is the boundary's *negative* pole and its cleanest contrast: it is a harness around
the **environment**, not around the agent, and it makes that explicit by refusing to touch the
environment's verifier. For the page this separates two things a loose reading of "harness"
merges - a wrapper that changes what the agent sees at reset/step (an environment layer) from a
wrapper that changes how the agent is driven (a host-side runtime). It also states the
discipline the page wants to copy: an added layer that leaves the goal predicate alone.
