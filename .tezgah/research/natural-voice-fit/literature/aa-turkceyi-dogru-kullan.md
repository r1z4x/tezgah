# Anadolu Ajansi, Turkceyi Dogru Kullan (corporate news-language guide)

**What it is and claims.** Newsroom rules that name the filler directly: avoid olmak, yapmak, etmek and never use olan or olup; avoid tane and adet as counters; cut the words that change nothing when removed; soz konusu is written separately.

**How it changes the skill.** The institutional Turkish basis for the filler-verb and counter lines of the marker table.

**Quality.** Primary institutional source, not peer-reviewed; for the book and the guides this is the field's standing authority.
