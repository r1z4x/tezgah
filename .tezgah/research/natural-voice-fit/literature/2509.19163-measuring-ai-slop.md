# Measuring AI "Slop" in Text (2509.19163)

**What it is.** A taxonomy of "slop" built from interviews with NLP, writing and
philosophy experts, plus a span-level annotation study over AI-generated text.

**What it claims.** There is no agreed definition of slop and no way to measure it;
expert-derived latent dimensions (coherence, relevance and the like) correlate with
binary slop judgements, but those judgements are only somewhat objective.

**What it gives us.** The reason our rubric must be multi-dimension and judge-based
rather than a single "is this slop" flag: a binary label is subjective even for
experts, so the measurement is a score per dimension with the disagreement reported.
It also legitimises naming patterns and quoting them, which is what the skill's
`Detect` job already does.

**How it would change the skill.** Ground the eval in named dimensions with anchors
instead of pass/fail intuition, and report inter-judge disagreement rather than
hiding it.
