# Hack-Verifiable Environments: Towards Evaluating Reward Hacking at Scale

- id: 2605.20744
- class: formal
- source: alphaxiv (report), confirmed by the keyword channel against openalex
- date: 2026-05-20
- reading: report read; the wrapper construction in full
- why read: the paper's core move is the one the layer is missing - stop *detecting*
  the failure after the fact and make it **verifiable by construction**.

## What it claims
- Wrap a base environment `E = (O, A_E, T, R)` into `E_HV = (O, A_HV, T_HV, R, H)`
  where the wrapper both adds actions and records hack events `H`, so exploiting a
  planted vulnerability is verifiable **deterministically** instead of by manual
  inspection or an LLM judge.
- Motivation stated against the alternatives: "Manual inspection is inherently
  unscalable" and "LLM-based evaluators ... can be unreliable and computationally
  expensive, introducing potential inaccuracies and resource burdens".

## Quotes (verbatim, from the alphaxiv report text fetched through orx)
- "Instead of analyzing hacking behavior post-hoc, the authors propose embedding
  detectable reward hacking opportunities directly into environments."
- "This approach is intended to make the exploitation of vulnerabilities verifiable
  by design, thereby enabling deterministic and automated measurement."

## What transfers to a hook-based harness
- **Plant the bait, then verify the bite mechanically.** A thrown-together demo or a
  stand-in that the harness itself created has a known expected value; if a claim
  reports that value as a property of the real system, the mismatch is decidable and
  needs no judge. This is the design the spec in `spec.md` proposes as the second
  control, and it is the only one of the three that can carry a hard refusal.
- **Judge-free detection is the goal, not a nicety.** Their argument against LLM
  judges is the same reason the gate's semantic seat refuses what it cannot decide:
  a relied-on judgement that cannot be reproduced is indistinguishable from a pass.

## What does not transfer
- Their environments are constructed for measurement; a user's repository is not,
  and planting bait in it would change what is being measured. The control has to be
  opt-in at the point where the layer already generates the fixture (the probe
  scripts and the research workspace), not everywhere.
