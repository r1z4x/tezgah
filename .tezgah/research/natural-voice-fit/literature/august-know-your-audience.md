# August, Lo, Smith, Reinecke, Know Your Audience (CHI 2024, arXiv 2403.04979)

**What it is and claims.** Three within-subjects studies (N=199/191/203): simplifying for a knowledgeable reader is a measurable loss - high-familiarity readers skipped the limitations section, 25% of all skips, and gained nothing from the plain version.

**How it changes the skill.** Audience adaptation is two-way: a developer or executive who knows the domain is harmed by plain-language flattening, so the brief has to say what the reader already knows, not only what they prefer.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
