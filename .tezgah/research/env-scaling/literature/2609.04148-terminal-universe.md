# 2609.04148 — Terminal-Universe: Turning Agent Trajectories into Scalable Terminal Environments

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04148 — Jie Wu, Zhenru Zhang, Beichen Zhang, Xuwu Wang, Yuhui
Su, Mouxiang Chen, Peng Wang, Zhihai Wang, Que Shen, Hao Zhou, An Yang, Fei Huang, Yujiu Yang,
Dayiheng Liu (Qwen Team, Alibaba; Tsinghua) — 2026-09-03, cs.AI / cs.CL. Report read in full;
the arXiv abstract was fetched as the second record.

## What it is
The 2026 successor in the SWE-smith/R2E-Gym line, and the one whose stated premise is the
post-training dependency itself: "environments are what agent post-training actually requires:
each can be re-queried into many verifiable tasks and provides execution feedback, whereas a
trajectory is a single frozen demonstration" (abstract). It inverts the usual direction -
instead of rolling agents out from environments, it reconstructs environments from recorded
agent trajectories by replaying the file operations in reverse, then completes the missing files
and dependencies with an agent, filters the workspace for sufficiency, and re-queries it for
intent-recovery tasks, single-workspace tasks, cross-workspace tasks and multi-round sessions,
each paired with an agent-authored `pytest` suite and filtered by a teacher rollout.

Numbers, the authors' own:
- "37.3k task-sufficient environments" from public terminal agent trajectories (abstract); the
  body reports 31,977 SFT demonstrations (25,386 Single-WS, 3,512 Cross-WS, 3,079 Multi-Round)
  totalling "approximately 1.42 billion training tokens".
- Sufficiency: deterministic replay alone yields "a 40.2% sufficiency rate for Terminal and 20.1%
  for SWE workspaces"; after agentic completion, "93.5% and 77.1%".
- Fine-tuning result: "Supervised fine-tuning of Qwen3.5-27B on this corpus improves single-round
  performance on Terminal-Bench 2.1 by 11.9 points and multi-round performance on EvoCode-Bench
  v2 MT@4 by 13.8 points" (abstract).
- Task-length profile: Multi-Round records had "median turns of 92, compared to 14 for Single-WS
  and 23 for Cross-WS".

## Why it is in this line
It is the 2026 successor the question asks for, and it is the cheapest version of the generation
step in this set - the raw material is trajectories the repository already has, not new
repositories. That matters to the adaptation answer, because a corpus of *recorded* agent
sessions is the one environment-adjacent asset this repository might already hold. It also
quantifies what reconstruction costs before it works: the replay-only sufficiency rate is 40.2%
and 20.1%, so more than half the raw material is dropped until a completion agent repairs it.

## Quality and limits
Formal: arXiv preprint, Qwen Team / Alibaba with two Tsinghua authors, no venue or peer review
visible in the record read. Every number is the authors' own, cited and not reproduced here. The
selection is teacher-model dependent (rollouts by Qwen3.7-Max in the Claude Code scaffold), so
the corpus inherits that model's success distribution.
