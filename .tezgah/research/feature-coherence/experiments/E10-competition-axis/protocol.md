# E10 - the competition axis neither analysis produced

## What changes
Nothing. Every arm of this line recorded the competition axis as not produced, for the
same reason: no competitor artifact was read. This experiment produces it for the two
audited surfaces - an admin user-management area - with the rubric's own discipline.

## Method
State the comparison basis first (task steps to grant and revoke access; feature
density of the user list; the read/write contract), then read at least three
comparable products' own documentation or demo at first hand, each with its URL and
the date read, and extract for each: how a user is created, how access is changed, how
it is revoked, and what the product makes visible about delivery (invite link, mail,
forced password change). Then compare against the two audited surfaces and name the
gaps each way.

## Predicts
At least three comparable products can be read at first hand and at least two of them
deliver an invitation credential through the product itself, a capability both audited
surfaces lack - so the axis lands as a finding with external evidence rather than as a
missing section.

## Falsification criterion
Fewer than three comparable surfaces readable at first hand, or none of them showing a
capability the audited surfaces lack, would make the axis a statement about the market
rather than a gap - and the analysis reports that.
