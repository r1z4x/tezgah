# ISO 17100:2015, translation services (linguistic specification)

**What it is and claims.** Terminology is a project-specification element inside project preparation (clause 4.6.3 Linguistic specification), and the standard requires the agreed specification to govern the work.

**How it changes the skill.** The professional frame that says a term list is part of the brief, not an afterthought: the skill asking for the audience and the format should ask for the term rail too.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
