# Google developer documentation style guide (voice and tone, highlights, translation)

**What it is and claims.** Developer register stated as too-informal / just-about-right / too-formal triples, with instructions to prefer the second person and the present tense and to cut marketing adjectives.

**How it changes the skill.** Gives the developer audience a citable feature list rather than the instruction to sound technical.

**Quality.** Grey: an organisation's own published guide, judged above; usable as a house standard, not as a measurement.
