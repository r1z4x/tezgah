# Semenov and Bojar 2022 (WMT) and Alam et al. 2021 - automated terminology consistency metrics

**What it is and claims.** Peer-reviewed metrics that measure how well a text adheres to required domain terminology, the first measuring consistency across a whole text rather than per sentence and reporting that it diverges from standard MT metrics while correlating with human assessment, the second proposing terminology-adherence metrics over five languages with terminology-targeted human evaluation and open-sourced code.

**How it changes the skill.** The check can be automated rather than judged: one measurement engine for every language, given a glossary, which is what makes the term axis cheap enough to run per language.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
