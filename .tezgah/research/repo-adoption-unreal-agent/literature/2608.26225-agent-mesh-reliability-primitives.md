## What it gives

The one source in this set whose whole yield is portable to a harness with no runtime.
From a failure study of a production agentic software-delivery platform (66,185 lines, 59
modules) over 147 numbered incidents spanning 81 runs, each with a measured cost and
mostly a mutation proof that reverting the fix reproduces the failure: all three
assumptions the service-mesh primitives (retry, timeout, error-rate circuit breaking)
rest on are violated in practice. Concretely — a loop of fifty-four consecutive
successful tool calls that no error-rate breaker could see; a progress signal computed
over an identifier that was **constant by construction**, which guaranteed a false trip on
the third repair round and drove one run from six of six components to three; twenty-one
events accumulated across six invocations of one delegation, making a correct and
demonstrably idempotent component unwinnable; a misrouted failure that woke five
components for a two-component fault; twelve incidents where the enforcement layer blocked
correct work, the most expensive costing 107 agent turns and zero accepted writes.

The two rules it derives are the portable part:

- **Identity adequacy** — in five separate subsystems an identity that failed to
  discriminate produced a confident wrong answer, and two subsystems derived the
  corrective rule independently.
- **Evidence adequacy** — a reliability decision may be taken only on evidence that is
  capable of moving, attributable to what it measures, and deterministic.

## What it means for tezgah

Evidence adequacy is a direct test for tezgah's own gate, its Stop rule and its counters:
a threshold over a signal that cannot move is not a check, and "constant by construction"
is the failure mode it names. Identity adequacy applies to the ledger's claim key
(`hooks/tezgah_integrity.py:1478`, an id derived from reply text), and it applies to the
surveyed repository's own Input contract — "a caller-supplied globally unique ID that
remains stable across redeliveries" (`README.md:11-12`) — whose discriminating power its
own code confines to one process lifetime (`README.md:33`, `harness/inbox/local.go`).
