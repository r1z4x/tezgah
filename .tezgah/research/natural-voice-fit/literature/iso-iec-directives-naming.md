# ISO/IEC Directives Part 2 (2021) - names, numbers, code and units

**What it is and claims.** Drafting rules that hold across languages: language versions shall be technically equivalent and structurally identical; the names of organisations and their abbreviations shall be written as used by those organisations; the three-digit grouping does not apply to the numbering of standards or to years; programming-language, pseudo-code and mark-up text follows the syntax of the language; and it is not permitted to modify a unit symbol, with language-specific abbreviations such as ppm avoided.

**How it changes the skill.** Four categories the skill can state as rules with a citation, including the structural-equivalence rule that keeps a translated document comparable to its original - which is the honest limit on how much an editor may restructure.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
