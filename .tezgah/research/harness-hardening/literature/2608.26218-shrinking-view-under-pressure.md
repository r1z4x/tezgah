# 2608.26218 — Same Model, Different Harness: Different Coding-Agent Results

Sydney Lewis. alphaXiv: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.26218 — 2026-08-26.
Read in full (alphaXiv report).

## What it is
Paired control-vs-treatment inside one harness ("Yuj"), model and task fixed. The
treatment: a **durable record kept in memory plus a dynamically shrinking model-visible
view** — older tool results mechanically shortened on a base-two half-life scale (age 4-7
→ 4096 chars, 32-63 → 512 chars) once the prompt reaches 50% of the window; a
**record-pattern stall detector** with fixed, pre-configured interventions (no model
calls); and command safeguards. Benchmarks: SWE-bench Verified (169), SWE-bench Pro
(316), FeatureBench (183); models Qwen3.6-35B-A3B plus three transfer models.

## The findings that bear on tezgah
- **Under tight context the treatment is large and significant**: mean per-task F2PF
  28% → 49% and complete solutions 43 → 72 on SWE-bench Verified at a 20,480-token window
  (both exact tests p < 0.0001).
- **At 262,144 tokens, where fewer than 1% of tasks hit the limit, the advantage largely
  disappears** (F2PF difference -0.3 pp, bootstrap interval covering zero).
- The treatment moves the "reading boundary" — the point where half the runs stop before
  making any source change — to **more than twice** the control's location.
- The shortening is **deterministic and auditable, with no additional model calls**, in
  contrast with model-based summarization.
- The frozen package transferred to three other models without retuning.

## Quality and limits
Formal-ish record (arXiv, single author, inconclusive affiliation). Its control was
itself validated against mini-SWE-agent v2.2.8 on 500 Verified tasks (87.6% agreement,
kappa 0.66). Effects are measured under pressure; the design cannot separate which of the
treatment's three mechanisms carries the gain.

## Why it is in this line
It is the strongest available evidence that a *mechanical, no-model-call* context policy
moves outcomes when context is tight and is near-worthless when it is not — the reading
tezgah's own budget measurement (E1: 76.2% of the budget spent at session_start) has to be
read against.
