# Google Cloud Translation glossaries - the vendor DNT mechanism and its counter-case

**What it is and claims.** The mechanism as a product feature: a glossary entry maps a term to itself so it survives translation (their example: Google Home stays Google Home), with stopwords for terms the glossary cannot enforce and case sensitivity as an option. The counter-case is stated in the same document: a borrowed word such as bouillabaisse may be overridden to fish stew, because a reader without the cultural context would not understand the loan.

**How it changes the skill.** Confirms the mechanism is a glossary, not a judgement call, and that the glossary itself has to carry the exceptions and the reading-level reasoning rather than leaving them to the model.

**Quality.** Institutional or vendor source read in session; prescriptive, not peer-reviewed.
