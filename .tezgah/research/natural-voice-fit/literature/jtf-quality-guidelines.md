# Japan Translation Federation quality guidelines (2018)

**What it is and claims.** The instrument, with arithmetic: an Untranslated accuracy error and a Terminology dimension whose subtypes are termbase violation, domain-term violation (their example: `named entity` must be 固有表現, not a katakana rendering) and internal inconsistency (戻り値 against 返り値). Severity points Critical 100 / Major 10 / Minor 1, category weights 2.0/1.5/1.0/0.5, score is error points divided by evaluated words times 1,000, with pass thresholds of 50 points per 1,000 source words into Japanese and 35 per 1,000 source characters out of it. It maps itself to MQM.

**How it changes the skill.** Gives the term axis the metric it should adopt - errors per 1,000 units, severity-weighted, with a pass threshold - so the experiment measures something the industry already recognises rather than a number we invented.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
