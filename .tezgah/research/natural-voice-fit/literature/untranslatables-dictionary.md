# Cassin, Apter, Lezra and Wood (eds.), Dictionary of Untranslatables (Princeton; the English edition of Vocabulaire europeen des philosophies)

**What it is and claims.** The established scholarly name and treatment for the phenomenon behind the user's question: concepts that resist translation, or must not be translated, handled language by language.

**How it changes the skill.** It replaces a folk term with a citable tradition, and it sets the expectation for the skill: the keep list is per concept and per language, and the honest answer for some concepts is that no equivalent exists.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an academic-press record.
