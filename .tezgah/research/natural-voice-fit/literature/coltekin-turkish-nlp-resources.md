# Coltekin, Dogruoz, Cetingolu, resources for Turkish NLP - a critical survey (LRE 2023, arXiv 2204.05042)

**What it is and claims.** The inventory and access conditions of Turkish corpora, including the Turkish Discourse Bank (about 400K words of the METU corpus with explicit connectives and their two arguments labelled, plus the TED-MDB sub-corpus), and the gaps.

**How it changes the skill.** It fixes which corpus represents which register, and makes the missing-link complaint measurable through labelled connective data.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record.
