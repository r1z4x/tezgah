# Regulated terminology: MedDRA, drug labelling and anatomical nomenclature

**What it is and claims.** Some terminology is binding rather than preferred. MedDRA tells users not to alter it: it is a standardised terminology with a predefined hierarchy, and ad hoc structural alteration compromises the standard. US drug labelling requires the required information to appear in English, with the same requirement extended to any foreign-language representation. Anatomical terminology is a controlled vocabulary titled in Latin, and the botanical code states that where a translated edition is ambiguous the English edition is definitive and machine translations should not be relied on.

**How it changes the skill.** The keep list has a floor the skill cannot negotiate: in some domains a term is regulated, and the right output is the regulated form plus, where needed, the local-language explanation beside it - never a replacement.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
