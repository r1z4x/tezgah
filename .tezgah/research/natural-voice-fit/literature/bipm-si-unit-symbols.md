# BIPM SI Brochure (9th edition) - unit symbols are not abbreviations

**What it is and claims.** Unit symbols are mandatory, printed in upright roman type regardless of the surrounding text, are mathematical entities rather than abbreviations, take no full stop except at the end of a sentence, take no plural, are not mixed with unit names in one expression, and may not be abbreviated further - `sec` for the second is not permissible. The decimal marker may be a point or a comma by language.

**How it changes the skill.** The unit category of the keep list, with the reason: a unit symbol is a mathematical object, so translating or pluralising it is an arithmetic error and not a style choice.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
