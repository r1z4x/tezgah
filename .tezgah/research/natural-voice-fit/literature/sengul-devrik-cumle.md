# Sengul, Avci, Kucuk 2017, inverted sentences in Turkish prose (TAKSAD 6(6))

**What it is and claims.** Inverted-sentence use across eight Turkish prose writers is higher in those writing on social subjects, and a 500-respondent survey shows the public divided - the same split as the linguists.

**How it changes the skill.** Inversion is a register marker with a measured distribution, not a defect: the skill must leave it alone or treat it as a register feature.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record.
