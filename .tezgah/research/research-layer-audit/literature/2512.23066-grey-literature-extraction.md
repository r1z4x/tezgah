## Claim

Grey literature "is essential to software engineering research as it captures
practices and decisions that rarely appear in academic venues", and the obstacle
is heterogeneous sources and APIs. The tool turns a topic prompt into
platform-specific queries, gathers from **GitHub, Stack Overflow and Google
Search**, then uses embedding-based semantic classifiers to filter and rank by
relevance. Two design commitments are named: reproducibility through
configuration-based settings, and every generated query being archived.

## Why it matters here

This is the concrete source list for the channel E4 found unnamed: for a harness
repository, the practice sources are issue threads, discussion/Q&A posts, docs
sites and release notes. The two commitments map onto what this layer already
knows how to do well elsewhere - configuration instead of one-off commands, and
an archived query next to its result - so the improvement is a *shape the layer
already has*, applied to a channel it never mentions.
