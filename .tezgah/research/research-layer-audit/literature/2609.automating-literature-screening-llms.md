## Claim

A framework that runs on a user-supplied corpus and integrates four stages in one
workflow: **relevance screening, structured information extraction, synthesis and
analysis**. It is benchmarked across three materials-science screening tasks
differing in scope and corpus size, with 11 proprietary and open-weight LLMs.
