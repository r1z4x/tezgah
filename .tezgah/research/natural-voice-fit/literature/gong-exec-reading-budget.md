# Gong Labs, do executives reply to cold email (vendor telemetry over 1M+ sales cycles)

**What it is and claims.** The executive reading budget: under three seconds to decide in an inbox, about nine seconds to read, 50-100 words in the body, and buzzwords measurably reduce replies.

**How it changes the skill.** Vendor telemetry, not a peer-reviewed measurement: usable as a length-and-density bound for the executive brief, not as a law.

**Quality.** Grey: an organisation's own published guide, judged above; usable as a house standard, not as a measurement.
