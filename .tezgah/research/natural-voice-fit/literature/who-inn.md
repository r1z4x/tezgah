# WHO International Nonproprietary Names

**What it is and claims.** Each INN is a unique globally recognised name for an active substance, chosen as a single name of worldwide acceptability, with national names now nearly always identical to it and its use normally required by national or international legislation.

**How it changes the skill.** The hard case in the medical register: a term that is not a style choice at all. The skill's keep list has to admit terms that are kept by law, and this is where the line is drawn.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
