# Necmiye Alpay, motamo (word-for-word) translation column, T24 (2024)

**What it is and claims.** Defines what smelling of translation means - word-for-word translation that cannot establish equivalence between contexts - and gives the list: aksiyon almak (take action) -> onlem almak / gerekeni yapmak; insanlara dokunmak (to touch) -> temas kurmak; fokus olmak -> odaklanmak; kavirlamak (cover) -> yorumlamak; vizyoner -> uzak goruslu; ilk kez tanistigimizda -> tanistigimizda.

**How it changes the skill.** The calque table comes ready-made from the field's most authoritative editor rather than from a blog list.

**Quality.** Primary institutional source, not peer-reviewed; for the book and the guides this is the field's standing authority.
