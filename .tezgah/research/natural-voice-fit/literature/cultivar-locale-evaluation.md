# Cultivar - a contrastive and locale-oriented translation benchmark (arXiv 2608.09766)

**What it is and claims.** Multilingual benchmarks are usually sourced in English and translated outward, which treats a language pair as the unit and ignores locale. The paper advocates source-contrastive and locale-specific evaluation and benchmarks 32 open-weight models, finding that specialised models are less robust, some models look overfit to the benchmark, and models translate US content better than content from other locales.

**How it changes the skill.** A language is not one target: the skill has to take a position for a locale, not for 'German' or 'Spanish', and the paper's design is the shape of the test - compare against material written in the target locale rather than against a translation of American source material.
