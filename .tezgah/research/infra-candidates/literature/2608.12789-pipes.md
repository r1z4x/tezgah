# PIPES: Securing Agent Perception with Provenance and Priors

- id: alphaXiv 2608.12789 (arXiv preprint), 2026-08-13
- authors: Sanjay Kariyappa, Severin Klingler, G. Edward Suh (NVIDIA)
- url: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.12789
- read: alphaXiv report, 2026-09-19

## Claim

Existing injection defences stop *instructions* hiding in *data*. PIPES names a
class they miss: **state-corruption attacks**, where attacker-controlled content
makes environmental claims that exceed its informational authority. The claim
stays data-shaped, so it does not read as an instruction - but it corrupts the
agent's perceived environment, which makes the agent's next attacker-desired
action look *justified to the action-level guardrail that then allows it*.

The defence screens at the **response boundary**, before the data enters the
reasoning context, on two axes:

- **semantic prior** - is this field saying the kind of thing this field is for?
- **provenance hierarchy** - does this source have the authority to assert it,
  and does it contradict a higher-authority source?

Assessment is deliberately decoupled from the action taken (remove, warn, block,
escalate), and it must hold against adaptive attackers who see the defence's
verdicts.

## Why it matters here

The mechanism is explicitly aimed at the gap tezgah leaves open on purpose.
`docs/gate.md` says an effect **inside** the root after a fetch is left to the
taint notice, "because it is recoverable from the snapshot"
(`hooks/tezgah_gate.py:732-738`), and the refusal at the sink covers writes whose
realpath leaves the root plus every shell effect class.

PIPES' point is that the in-root write is exactly where a state-corruption attack
lands: the fetched page asserts that the config/route/schema is X, the agent
edits the in-root file to X, and the gate sees an ordinary in-root write with no
untrusted channel in the target. tezgah's taint notice fires, but nothing
refuses.

Two design constraints come with it, and both are already tezgah doctrine:
fail-open is required (a screen that cannot run must not block), and the screen
must not be an LLM asked "is this safe" - tezgah measured that primitive at
precision 0.167 on its own adversarial set and left the seat empty
(`docs/gate.md`, "The seat a semantic rule would take, and why it is empty").
