# Zeyrek and Basibuyuk 2019, Turkish discourse connectives (ACL W19-3308)

**What it is and claims.** 180 Turkish discourse connectives with a measured sense distribution (Expansion 36%, Contingency 24.4%, Comparison 22.3%, Temporal 17.3%) and the top-frequency list.

**How it changes the skill.** Connective density has a Turkish inventory to count against, which makes one of the user's complaints - missing links between clauses - computable rather than a matter of opinion.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
