# Nieth et al. 2026, how human-like are LLMs - a register-aware linguistic analysis (arXiv 2605.23651)

**What it is and claims.** Register-aware human-likeness measured as maximum mean discrepancy over 67 Biber features against a human corpus of the same register, with code released.

**How it changes the skill.** This is the audience-fit instrument for our experiment: it compares an edited draft to a human corpus of the target register instead of asking a judge for an opinion.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
