## Claim

A **Multivocal Literature Review** adds grey literature - "blog posts, videos and
white papers" - to the formal literature, and it needs its own guidelines because
"several phases of MLRs differ from those of traditional SLRs, for instance with
respect to the **search process** and **source quality assessment**". Derived by
extending Kitchenham and Charters' SLR guidelines across planning, conducting and
reporting.

## Why it matters here

This is the named standard for the gap E4 measures. The layer's citation rule -
four scholarly databases - is an SLR rule applied to questions that are mostly
MVLR questions ("should this rule exist", "which harness shape wins"), and the
missing phase is exactly the one the standard flags as different: source *quality
assessment* for grey sources (who published it, is it dated, is it a primary
report or a re-blog), which the workspace cannot express because it has no
source-class field at all.
