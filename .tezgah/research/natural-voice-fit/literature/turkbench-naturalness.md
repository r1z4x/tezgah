# TurkBench, a Turkish LLM benchmark (ACL SIGTURK 2026, arXiv 2601.07020)

**What it is and claims.** Its appendix states the naturalness criterion verbatim: accuracy, grammatical well-formedness and cultural sensitivity, with Comprehensibility, Fluency and Ambiguity defined as whether a native speaker would find the sentence clear, smooth and idiomatic, and whether it is vague enough to prevent a consistent reading. Limitation: its items cover formal, academic and newspaper register.

**How it changes the skill.** This is the measurable Turkish naturalness criterion our rubric should be built on - clear, smooth, idiomatic, unambiguous - instead of a home-made scale.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record.
