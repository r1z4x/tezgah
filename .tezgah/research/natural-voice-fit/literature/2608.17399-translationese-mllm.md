# Translationese in the Generations of Multilingual LLMs (2608.17399)

**What it is.** A study asking whether multilingual LLM output resembles
*translationese* - the traces a text carries when it was translated - and how
model-produced translationese differs from direct translation.

**What it claims.** Established indicators of translated text are applied to
state-of-the-art multilingual models across five languages; the paper's premise is
that model generations may be internal translations from English and therefore carry
translationese.

**What it gives us.** The mechanism behind the Turkish complaints the user named
(devrik cümle, anlam kopukluğu, translated-sounding verbs): a model writing Turkish
may be translating from an English plan, and translationese has measurable
indicators - so a Turkish marker set can be built from the translation-studies
indicator list rather than from taste.

**How it would change the skill.** Add a Turkish (and generally non-English) section
whose markers are translation-induced, not merely "AI-flavoured": calqued verb
phrases (`adreslemek`, `implemente etmek`), English word order in subordinate
clauses, nominalisation where Turkish prefers a verb, and discourse connectives
carried over instead of the Turkish ones a native writer would use.

**Quality.** Formal (arXiv 2608.17399, verified against OpenAlex 2026-08-18, 0 cites
at read time - new, so treat its indicator list as a lead to check, not a settled
finding).
