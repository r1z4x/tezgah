# Apple identity guidelines: never translate the mark, never transliterate it

**What it is and claims.** Verbatim: keep the name Apple Arcade in English even inside text in another language, do not translate the words Apple, Arcade or Apple Arcade, do not transliterate them, such as rendering the name in katakana in Japanese, and do not translate the name in spoken promotions. The App Store rules say the same for the service mark. Trademarks are excluded from what a translation may change.

**How it changes the skill.** The strongest form of the keep rule: not merely do not translate, but do not transliterate and do not respell, which is exactly what a marker set hunting for foreign-looking words would get wrong.

**Quality.** Institutional or vendor source read in session; prescriptive, not peer-reviewed.
