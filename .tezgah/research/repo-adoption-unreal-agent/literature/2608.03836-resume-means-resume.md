## What it gives

A named vocabulary for a question that is otherwise answered in prose. The RESUME
CONTRACT states six properties over a persistence API — **prefix continuation, effect
exactly-once, fork determinism, checkpoint validity, consume-once, recovery
determinism** — plus fork-intent and liveness obligations. It is machine-checked: a TLA+
model checked exhaustively with verdicts unchanged at scaled bounds (7.4×10⁶ distinct
states) and a reference conjunction proved unbounded over 196 TLAPS obligations; a
39-cell fault matrix separates the properties (five independent of the conjunction of the
others, and consume-once splits).

## The measured half

Across five deployed agent workflow frameworks at pinned releases, "no two probed
frameworks share a conformance profile". Two named failures matter here: LangGraph 1.2.9
records a second resume value, never consults it, and re-executes durably recorded work
after a real SIGKILL — "exactly-once across interrupts, at-least-once across crashes, on
one API"; CrewAI 1.15.2 re-executes completed effect-bearing methods against its written
claim. And the failure with the sharpest edge: **consume-once holds sequentially but
fails under concurrent delivery** — k processes resuming one parked interrupt fire the
gated effect k times, saturation 1.0 in 36 of 40 cells and never below 0.933 on both
durable backends, with the admitting window tracking the gated node's own execution time.
The repair is a gate that claims consumption in the shared store before any node
executes — i.e. a shared durable store, an engine component.

## What it means for tezgah

tezgah's hook invocations are serial per host, so it does not face the concurrent-delivery
cell this paper measures. The action is therefore **not** to acquire the store — that
would still need the read-path gate — but to adopt the six-property vocabulary as a way
to say honestly which properties tezgah does and does not have. A research line claiming
"exactly-once" without saying which of the six it means is the defect this note exists to
prevent.
