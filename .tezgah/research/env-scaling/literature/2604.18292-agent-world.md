Longxiang Liu, Shijue Huang, Zhenyu Li, Yang Zhao, Xiaoshuai Song, Xiaoxi Li, Jiajie Jin, Yutao

Zhicheng Dou (Renmin University of China; ByteDance Seed) — 2026-04-20, cs.AI / cs.CL. Report
read in full; the arXiv abstract was fetched as the second record.

## What it is
A self-evolving training arena built on synthesized real-world tool environments: an
Agentic Environment-Task Discovery pipeline mines topic-aligned databases from the web, generates
executable Python tool interfaces with unit tests, builds a 20/50/2,000-node environment
taxonomy, and synthesizes verifiable tasks two ways (graph-based tool chains and programmatic
scripts). Training is multi-environment RL with GRPO; a self-evolving arena re-synthesizes
evaluation tasks each round, diagnoses weak environments from failures, and expands them.

Numbers, the authors' own - and this is the source with an explicit environment-count curve:
- "1,978 environments, 19,822 tools" with "average of over 20 interaction turns" per task.
- Scaling: "Increasing the number of training environments from 0 to 1,978 consistently improved
  downstream agentic tool-use performance across representative domains ... The average score
  more than doubled (from 18.4% to 38.5%, an increase of 20.1 points)."
- The curve's shape, in the authors' own wording: "Performance gains were most pronounced from 10
  to 100 environments and again from 100 to 500 environments, indicating that initial and
  moderate-scale environment expansion rapidly improves the coverage of critical interaction
  patterns. Diminishing, though still positive, returns were observed from 500 to 2,000
  environments".
- Self-evolution rounds: Agent-World-14B on τ²-Bench/BFCL-V4/MCP-Mark "improved from
  60.2%/52.4%/29.5% to 65.4%/55.8%/38.1% after two rounds"; "Second-round gains were smaller but
  remained positive".
- Benchmark level: Agent-World-8B "achieved 61.8% on τ²-Bench, 51.4% on BFCL V4, and 8.9% on
  MCP-Mark, surpassing EnvScaler-8B and ScaleEnv-8B".

## Why it is in this line
It supplies the scaling relationship the question asks for in its most usable form: a measured
count-versus-capability curve, with the elbow at a few hundred environments and diminishing but
still positive returns out to 1,978. It also demonstrates the self-evolution loop that the
counter-argument sources replace with selection - and its own second-round gains shrink, which
is the same saturation the counter-argument sources attack from the other side.

## Quality and limits
Formal: arXiv preprint, Renmin University and ByteDance Seed, full author list, no venue or peer
review visible in the record read. Every number is the authors' own, cited and not reproduced
here. The environment-count curve is over this system's own synthesized environments and its own
task suite; it is not a general law, and the report gives no seed count beside it.
