# Mozilla localization style guide - text that should not be translated

**What it is and claims.** The canonical rule set: variables are never translated; command keys are normally not localised; access keys are decided per locale; trademarks are always used in their original wording and a product name is never translated by its meaning (their example: do not render Firefox with words meaning fire and fox), with trademark law as the rationale and the registered Chinese characters as the stated exception. It also requires terminology consistency against an approved term base.

**How it changes the skill.** The developer-register keep list, stated as rules with a reason, and the source for the skill's rule that identifiers, keys and product names are not prose.

**Quality.** Institutional or survey source read in session; prescriptive or descriptive, not peer-reviewed measurement.
