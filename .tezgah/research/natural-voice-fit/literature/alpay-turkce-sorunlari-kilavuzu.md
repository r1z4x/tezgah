# Necmiye Alpay, Turkce Sorunlari Kilavuzu (Metis, 5th ed. 2015; full text read)

**What it is and claims.** The primary Turkish editorial authority, alphabetical with cross-references, and it gives the rule, the reason and the replacement. Load-bearing entries: devrik cumle is explicitly NOT a defect (dusuk cumle is the defective one; devrik cumle is a sentence whose predicate has moved, not a broken one); redundant third-person pronouns forced by English or French; a pronoun placed before its referent; `X altinda` used for a period or a condition where `X doneminde / kosullarinda / yonetiminde` is meant; `ki` inflation; the double passive (gorulebilinecegi unutulmamalidir -> gorulebilecegi); `olarak` inflation; bilim dili and kesinleyici dil, where scholarly Turkish is expected to leave doubt rather than assert; and DIL DUZEYI, the register concept itself, naming ozenli, resmi, ciddi, bilimsel, gundelik as levels of language.

**How it changes the skill.** This is the Turkish core of the skill: not a marker list but rules with replacements, and the citable basis for a per-register voice. It also forbids the one rule a naive Turkish marker list would invent - penalising inverted sentences.

**Quality.** Primary institutional source, not peer-reviewed; for the book and the guides this is the field's standing authority.
