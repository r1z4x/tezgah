# Plaza Turkcesi in use - a 470-person survey and the attestation of the colloquial name

**What it is and claims.** A survey of 470 people reported in 2023 gives the expressions in use (`toplanti set etmek`, `brainstorming yapmak`, `discuss etmek`), the most disliked (`toplanti set etmek`, `task acmak`) and the sector split (technology and IT 34.7 percent, finance 24.9, advertising and marketing 19.8, FMCG 10.2), and records the reason speakers give for using it: there is no exact Turkish equivalent. Separately, `plazma dili` - the user's form of the name - is attested in a school magazine hosted on the Turkish Ministry of National Education domain, where a panel on language corruption covered `plazma dili` alongside what language and culture are.

**How it changes the skill.** It answers the user's own claim with speakers' own reason: the deliberate loanword is a register, and the survey's most-disliked list is where the register turns into a defect even for the people who use it.

**Quality.** Institutional or survey source read in session; prescriptive or descriptive, not peer-reviewed measurement.
