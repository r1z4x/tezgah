# False friend (the mechanism, from Koessler and Derocquigny 1928)

**What it is and claims.** A word in two languages that looks or sounds alike but differs in meaning, with the loanword mechanism that produces it: a borrowed word narrows or shifts meaning in the host language (German Gift against English gift; the Quebec French and European French split on eventuellement).

**How it changes the skill.** The failure mode behind the worst case of a wrong translation: the term that looks right. It is the reason a keep-or-translate decision cannot be made from word shape, and why MQM classes it as a mistranslation rather than a style choice.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an institutional framework.
