# Translationese as a rational response to translation task difficulty (arXiv 2603.12050)

**What it is and claims.** Translationese is explained as a response to the cognitive load of translating: the paper tests whether observable translationese can be predicted from quantifiable measures of translation task difficulty, and measures translationese as a segment-level probability assigned by an automatic classifier. It also names the standing explanations - interference, simplification, socio-cultural variables and language-pair effects - and the language-pair effect is the one that matters here: the size of the effect is a property of the pair, not of the target language alone.

**How it changes the skill.** It says the defect is proportional to the distance between the language the plan was formed in and the language the text is written in, so the skill's fix has to know the pair, and it gives a per-segment measurement instead of a document verdict.
