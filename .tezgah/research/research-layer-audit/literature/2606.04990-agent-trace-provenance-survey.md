## Claim

The field needs a shared vocabulary before it can audit anything, and the survey
supplies one: a **six-dimensional taxonomy** over trace sources, evidence and
execution units, provenance relations, granularity and timing, representation
forms, and trust functions - built on the W3C PROV-DM tradition and specialised
for agents. It names four design tensions, one of which is stated as the
chronological-logging-versus-semantic-evidence tension: a log records *that*
something happened, an evidence link records *what it supports*.

## Why it matters here

tezgah's research layer writes the first half and not the second. `results.jsonl`
is a chronological record; `claims.jsonl` carries an "evidence" pointer that
resolves to a path, not to a state of the world. The survey's vocabulary is what
a reviewer of this layer would use to say the same thing: the trust function of
`claims.jsonl` is *attestation*, while the layer believes it is *support*.
