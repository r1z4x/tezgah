# Accademia della Crusca, Consulenza linguistica (1,744 published answers)

**What it is and claims.** An academy answering usage questions per item, indexed by tags that name exactly this problem: calco semantico, anglismo, forestierismi, lingue speciali, burocrazia - including entries on proattivo and on magnificare as a calque on English.

**How it changes the skill.** The per-term authority the keep-or-translate rule needs, and proof that some languages adjudicate calques one item at a time rather than by rule. The skill cites the entry; it does not invent the verdict.

**Quality.** Institutional or community source read in session; the field's own authority, not a measurement.
