# Toral 2019, post-editese - an exacerbated translationese (ACL W19-6627)

**What it is and claims.** Machine-translated text that a human has corrected is more simplified and normalised than a human translation from scratch, with lower lexical diversity: the translation smell does not disappear when a human edits it, it gets worse.

**How it changes the skill.** Removes the excuse that a human pass over model output settles the matter; the check has to be on the text, not on who touched it last.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record.
