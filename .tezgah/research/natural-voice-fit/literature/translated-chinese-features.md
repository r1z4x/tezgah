# Hu, Li and Kubler 2018, detecting syntactic features of translated Chinese (ACL workshop on stylistic variation)

**What it is and claims.** A comparable corpus of 500 original Chinese texts against 500 human-translated ones, four genres, about a million words each: translated Chinese overuses pronouns (subject-position and genitive), determiners, the particle de after noun-phrase modifiers, and conjunctions where original Chinese prefers the enumeration comma. Classification accuracy by feature set: character n-grams 95.3, POS n-grams 93.9, subtree depth 92.2. Its related work is a small cross-language survey with accuracies: Italian 86, Spanish, English POS trigrams 98 and function words 96.

**How it changes the skill.** Confirms the marker families generalise in kind (pronouns, function words, connectives) while the specific forms are language-specific, and that function-word and POS distributions are the portable instruments - the same ones the translationese paper measured, so the rail can reuse one measurement engine per language.

**Quality.** Formal: a standard, a peer-reviewed paper or an academic-press record.
