# 2609.11677 — Ecdysis: Efficient and Effective Training of Runtime Harnesses for LLM Agents

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11677 — Ruiqing Yue, Yu Cui, Zhuoyu Sun, Sicheng Pan,
Xianhong Xue, Tingyu Li, Ting Li, Wenzhuo Zhu, Yi Chen, Yifei Liu, Baohan Huang, Zhe Cui,
Haibin Zhang, Cong Zuo — 2026-09-10, cs.SE / cs.AI. Abstract fetched through the arXiv API and
the paper's first pages read through the alphaXiv mirror
(https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.11677).

## What it is
Ecdysis attacks the failure-diagnosis bottleneck in harness evolution: an observed failure can
reflect either a deficiency of the *model* or a deficiency of the *harness*, and optimizing
against individual failures drifts into model-specific accommodation. Its two mechanisms are
batch-level cross-instance failure aggregation - recurrence across independent task instances
is treated as an inductive bias toward systematic harness deficiency, not as proof of cause -
and Failure-Driven Collaborative Refinement (FDCR), in which Analyst, Critic and Engineer roles
iterate over the collected evidence and a Moderator synthesizes a structured modification
specification the coding agent then applies.

Reported: up to a 1.84x speedup in harness training against existing harness-evolution methods,
while improving the reasoning accuracy of the resulting harnesses by 18.56%; harnesses trained
this way also generalize across LLMs and consume fewer inference tokens. The full text adds a
preliminary measurement this line cares about: across six Qwen3 model-domain combinations,
removing all harness layers averages 29.72%, a fixed human-configured harness 50.28%, and
serial failure-driven modification only 43.33%.

## Why it is in this line
Ecdysis supplies the vocabulary the boundary page needs for the *inside* of the harness layer:
"model-specific accommodation" against "harness-level repair". Its preliminary numbers also
separate the layers in the direction the page argues - the harness is what moves a fixed model
from 29.72% to 50.28%, while a naive editor of that harness lands below a hand configuration.
The 1.84x and 18.56% figures are the efficient half of the same claim.
