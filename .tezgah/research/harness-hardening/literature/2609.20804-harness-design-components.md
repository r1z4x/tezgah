# 2609.20804 — An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents

Fan, Zhang, Ma, Hu, Wang, Song, Liu, Zamani, Wang (UMass Amherst, Emory, UNC Charlotte, Zoom).
alphaXiv: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.20804 — 2026-09-17. Read in full (alphaXiv report).

## What it is
Component-level ablation of a fixed ReAct harness — planning, action space, context
management varied independently — over 4 models (Nemotron-3 30B/120B/550B,
Mistral-Medium-3.5-128B), SWE-Bench Verified + Terminal-Bench 2.1, 176 settings,
exact McNemar with Benjamini-Hochberg.

## The findings that bear on tezgah
- **Context management is the component whose value is conditional on pressure.** The
  managed-vs-unmanaged success gap falls from 35.7 pp at 32k tokens to 2.7 pp at 128k;
  unmanaged runs overflow on 78.7% of SWE-Bench tasks at 32k, 8.7% at 128k. Managed tiers
  record no overflow failures. Trajectory analysis: management "keeps agents running
  longer" rather than changing their workflow.
- **Rule-based elision first, summarization only after: T4 is comparable in success at
  lower cost in 7 of 8 panels.** T2's recoverable external storage plus a `recall_event`
  tool gives **no reliable accuracy benefit over T1** (15 wins, 14 losses, 3 ties across
  32 matched comparisons) and recall use is rare — "external recovery adds complexity that
  most evaluated models do not exploit".
- **Planning is capability-dependent**: +11.6 pp success for the weakest model at added
  cost; for the strongest models it changes accuracy little while **cutting cost ~30%**,
  mostly by removing redundant post-edit verification steps.
- **Action space**: structured tools +15.0 pp for the weakest model; the strongest model
  is better and much cheaper with bash-only (-53% cost).

## Quality and limits
Formal paper record (arXiv id + venue authors). Limits the authors state: each task run
once, small Terminal-Bench, ablations only at 128k with T4, the action-space comparison
bundles tools with prompts/validation/state tracking, model size is an imperfect
capability proxy.
