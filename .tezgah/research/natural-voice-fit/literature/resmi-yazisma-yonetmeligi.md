# Resmi Yazismalarda Uygulanacak Usul ve Esaslar Hakkinda Yonetmelik (RG 31151, 2020) with Diyanet, Anadolu and KTUE reprints

**What it is and claims.** Turkish formal register is regulated, not taste: `arz` vs `rica` follows hierarchy, real persons take `Saygilarimla`, TDK is the named authority, foreign words need a gloss, and the institutional style ends every sentence but the last in `-dir/-dir` with no personal verbs. This grounds what a model does by habit - the `-mektedir` inflation and impersonal nominalisation - in an actual convention, which is why applying it to product prose reads wrong.

**How it changes the skill.** The marker list can say when the formal register is correct (official correspondence) and that applying it elsewhere is the defect.

**Quality.** Grey: an organisation's own published guide, judged above; usable as a house standard, not as a measurement.
