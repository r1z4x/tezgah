## What it is
The purest statement of the counter-argument, in the authors' own words: "Recent pipelines
generate such environments in bulk, which moves the bottleneck from how many exist to what is
inside each one" (abstract). Echoverse compiles a written specification into a stateful
application (FastAPI, SQLite, React), grows a task corpus whose references are read out of the
database at generation time, and grades outcomes against the database rather than against a
screenshot judge. Its loop reads every graded rollout twice: once as a repair signal for the
environment, its tasks and its verifier, and once as training signal for the model. A design
rule this repository's own rule set mirrors: "Goals are never weakened to raise pass rates."

Numbers, the authors' own:
- "Trained on twelve such environments, a 9B model improves from 36.5% to 67.1% across fourteen
  evaluation splits, within fourteen points of the much larger frontier model that taught it."
- Depth versus count, on the same domains: "shallow environments push live-site accuracy below
  the base model (80.0 → 75.0) while deep ones raise it (80.0 → 85.0 and 48.0 → 65.0)".
- Repair effect: "repairing a single environment lifts the model trained on it from 16.2% to
  38.5%".
- RL, with a grounded verifier plus a dense per-step judge: "raises held-out score from 58.8% to
  68.0%".
- Construction quality gates: a build phase continues until "at least 95% of claims pass", and a
  corpus phase until "95% of the corpus passes verification".

## Why it is in this line
It is the counter-argument at its most operational: the environment's *interior* - depth,
capability targeting, and a repair loop driven by graded rollouts - is named as the bottleneck
once generation is cheap, and each of the three properties is measured separately. The pair of
numbers that matter most here are the shallow-versus-deep split (the same domains, accuracy
moving 80.0 → 75.0 one way and 80.0 → 85.0 the other) and the single-environment repair (16.2% to
38.5%), because both are *quality* interventions with no new environment added.

## Quality and limits
Formal: arXiv preprint, Microsoft Research, full author list, code link, no venue or peer review
visible in the record read. Every number is the authors' own, cited and not reproduced here. The
evaluation splits are this paper's own fourteen; the corpus is a fixed released set of four
environments plus their generators, and the report gives no seed count for the headline training
run.
