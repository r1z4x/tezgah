# Biber 2012, register as a predictor of linguistic variation (CLLT 8(1))

**What it is and claims.** Register is measurable: lexical bundles in conversation and academic prose overlap about 3%, and the variation is described as rates per million words over grammatical features rather than as adjectives about tone.

**How it changes the skill.** An audience section can name features and their directions instead of telling the writer to sound executive.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
