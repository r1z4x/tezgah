## Claim, with its exact numbers

A **pre-registered** reproducibility audit of an agent-produced artifact
literature, and the four rungs it separates are the useful part: publicly
available, runnable, signal-producing, and semantically confirmed. Search over
2023-2026 with dual screening yields a 104-paper consensus corpus, of which **59
papers (56.7%) have a publicly reachable artifact**. The study then executes an
18-paper sample at two levels, plus 102 cases of an anchor benchmark, with
patched-counterfactual verdicts on 30 signal-producing cases and
matched-negative-control verdicts on 19.

## Why it matters here

The four rungs apply to this layer's own output, and the layer stops at the first
two: a line's `results.jsonl` is present and runnable-in-principle; nothing
establishes that it produced a signal (E1 P5: an empty results file passes), and
nothing establishes that a claim is semantically supported by the artifact it
names (E1 P4/P6). The pre-registration plus matched-negative-control design is
also the template for auditing a *checker* rather than an artifact, which is what
E1 does with its 2/2 control.
