# Microsoft per-language localization style guides (111 locales)

**What it is and claims.** Where the vendor's keep rules actually live: inside each language guide, not in one global list. The Turkish guide (52 pages) states that trademarks are not localised unless local law requires an approved translated form, version numbers are not translated, placeholders must keep their original positions, product names are never abbreviated, and `&`, `@`, `#` are kept only inside code, tags and shortcuts, with acronyms mostly used as-is and only USA/ABD and OVR/UYZ localised.

**How it changes the skill.** A rail is per language and per product, and the skill should expect its keep list to come from such a source rather than from its own taste - and it should look in the language guide, which is where the rules are.

**Quality.** Institutional or vendor source read in session; prescriptive, not peer-reviewed.
