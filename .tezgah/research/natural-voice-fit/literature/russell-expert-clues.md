# Russell et al. 2025, people who frequently use ChatGPT are accurate AI-text detectors (arXiv 2501.15654)

**What it is and claims.** Five untrained expert annotators beat every open detector and match the best commercial one; the paper releases a 300-article dataset, the clue taxonomy, a guidebook and a humanizing prompt. The taxonomy groups the clues humans use: vocabulary, sentence structure, grammar and punctuation, originality, quotes, clarity.

**How it changes the skill.** Our marker list should be organised by that taxonomy, and the experiment's rubric can be built from it instead of from taste. It also says a trained reader is a better instrument than a detector.

**Quality.** Formal: peer-reviewed or an arXiv record, verified against two sources.
